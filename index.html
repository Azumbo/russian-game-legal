Privacy Policy — Russian game (Ring Maze)

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Contact: azumbogames@icloud.com

Summary

Russian game is an Apple Watch puzzle. We do not sell your personal data. Most gameplay data stays on your device.

Data on your device

The app stores locally (for example via UserDefaults): level progress and unlock state; settings such as haptics, tilt sensitivity, and language; and optional daily reminder preference.

Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, Apple may process leaderboard scores and achievements under Apple’s Game Center terms. We do not operate a separate account system.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your full payment card details.

Analytics

If analytics are enabled in a given build, events are limited to gameplay and product metrics (for example level start/finish and purchase funnel). No advertising ID is required for core play.

Motion

The app uses Watch motion sensors to control the ball. Motion data is processed on-device for gameplay and is not uploaded by us as a personal profile.

Children’s privacy

The app is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do.
